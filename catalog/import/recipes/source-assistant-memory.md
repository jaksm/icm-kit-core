# Another assistant's memory or chat history

The source with the most stale material and the most statements about the owner that they never
made: an assistant's summary of a person is an inference, not a fact. So nothing from here enters
as `authoritative`. Each statement about the owner becomes a question to them, in rounds of about
four; their answer is what is written, with today's date.

Ask for the export the product offers (most have one under privacy or data controls). It may take
hours to arrive and the download link expires. Chat history is large: inventory by conversation
title and month, and open only what they point at.
