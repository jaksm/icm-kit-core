# Any other source

**What must hold:** you can list what is there without opening it; you can open one item at a time;
nothing in the source is changed by reading it; the owner can see the item you are asking about.

**Find out:** is there an export, and does it keep dates and titles; do links in it expire; where
would secrets be (attachments, screenshots, settings pages); is any of it someone else's.

**Proof:** the inventory matches what the owner sees in the source, counted.

**When to say no:** a source that can only be read by logging in as the owner in a browser you
drive, with their password. They log in; you read what they show you.
