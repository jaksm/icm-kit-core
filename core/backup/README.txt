This stick can rebuild your ICM on a new computer.

It holds your encryption key. Anyone who has this stick AND your key passphrase can read everything.
Keep it somewhere safe, away from the computer it backs up.

On a new computer: open your AI coding agent in this folder and say "read AGENT.md and restore my ICM".
Or run restore-mac.sh (Mac) or restore-windows.ps1 (Windows) yourself.

Refresh it now and then from inside your ICM:   bash core/backup/make-usb.sh <path to this stick>
